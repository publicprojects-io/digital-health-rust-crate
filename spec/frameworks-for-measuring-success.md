Frameworks for Measuring Success
Organizations rarely build digital care metrics from scratch. They rely on globally recognized evaluation frameworks to structure their data:
• The WHO Digital Health Assessment Framework: Focuses heavily on equity, feasibility, and safety across national health deployments.
• The RE-AIM Framework: Evaluates digital interventions across five specific dimensions: Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, and Maintenance.
• The ISO/TS 82304-2 Standard: A global framework specifically developed to assess the quality, data security, and clinical reliability of health and wellness applications.
